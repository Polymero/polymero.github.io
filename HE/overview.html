Overview

To ensure ongoing quality of our services, we conduct strict examination on the participants of selected job positions. Intergalactic Diplomat (IgD) is one of them. As IgD on behalf of Hyperspatial Engineering you will conduct business outside of the information sphere of central command or of any of our outposts. It is therefore crucial that our deployed IgDs are capable of making the right decisions according to our guidelines. In order to test your capabilities we have established an examination encompassing the following subjects:

  • Contextual Awareness
    Being aware of and understanding your surroundings is one of the most critical aspects of working as IgD.
    This module will test your ability to piece together scraps of information that might not be found close by.
  • Communication
    Outside of our information sphere, communications must be encrypted to ensure confidentiality.
    This module will test your ability to find and/or decrypt hidden messages.
  • Cosmorithmatics
    A basic understanding of cosmorithmatics, physics, and astronomy is a requirement for extrasphere positions.
    This module will test your ability to find solutions through mathematics and logic.
  • Data Analysis
    All is data, and data is all. Hence it is advantageous to be familiar with various analysis techniques.
    This module will test your ability to process and analyse data in order to filter out important messages and/or information.
  • Intergalactic Languages
    Language is a common found, yet complex, form of ecryption. Some basic linguistical skills can reveal possible origins and discrepancies.
    This module will test your ability to find linguistical patterns in some of the (lesser) known languages of the Virgo Cluster.
Please complete the modules below keeping the following in mind:
  • Cooperation with fellow participants is allowed, and advised. Sign up your team in our Discord!
  • The use of external decryption tools/software is allowed, but not necessary. They are unlikely to be of any help.
  • For the Data Analysis module, it is advised to use processing and visualisation tools/software (e.g. Python or Excel)
  • The whole examination relies on logic, reason and insight, brute-force techniques are not necessary.
  • The examination is meant to be challenging (and educational), do not expect to complete it any time soon.
  • Making notes of discoveries and your progress might come in handy!
  • Please contact eduardiepema(at)gmail.com (or check our Discord) for hints and guidance, especially if you think you might lack required knowledge and/or skills to solve a question.
All questions are labelled with their respective module, ordinal number, and title.
Each question within a module will lead you to the next through an answer that can be used as /HE-IgD/insert_answer_here.html.
